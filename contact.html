Frequently asked

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. Northform Construction is licensed in Oregon under CCB #204512, carries $2M in general liability coverage, and is bonded. Certificates of insurance are provided with every contract.

What size projects do you take on?

Renovations typically start around $50,000. New homes and commercial projects range from $400,000 to roughly $6M. We will tell you honestly if a project is too small or outside our expertise.

Can you work with my architect or designer?

Yes. We regularly build from other firms’ drawings and can join the team during preconstruction to review constructability and cost. We can also provide design services in-house.

Do you handle permits?

We prepare and submit permit applications for the work we contract, and coordinate inspections with the local jurisdiction. Where a project requires a separate architect or engineer of record, we coordinate their submissions.

How quickly can you start?

Preconstruction can usually begin within two to three weeks. Site mobilization depends on permitting and current workload, typically eight to fourteen weeks after a signed contract.
