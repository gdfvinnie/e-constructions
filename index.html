The practice

Construction is a
coordination discipline.

Founded in Portland in 2009, Northform is a 34-person general contractor working across residential, commercial, renovation, and civil projects in Oregon and Southwest Washington.

Buildings are rarely made by a single hand. We connect the people, drawings, decisions, and site conditions that shape a project, so the work can move forward with fewer surprises.

Our team manages preconstruction, permitting, scheduling, procurement, and site supervision in-house. That means one accountable team from the first feasibility conversation through the final walkthrough, with clear reporting at every stage.

Why choose us

How we
work.

Five commitments that shape every Northform project, from a basement remodel to a multi-storey workplace.

Licensed, bonded, and insured in Oregon. CCB #204512.

  • Careful planning

    We map the brief, constraints, permits, and sequence before commitments are made on site — and we price the work we can actually deliver.

  • Clear communication

    A named project manager, weekly progress reports, and decisions documented in plain language, so you always know what changed and why.

  • Safety-minded working practices

    Site-specific safety plans, documented orientations, and daily checks. Every crew on a Northform site has stop-work authority.

  • Quality-focused delivery

    Work is checked against approved drawings at each milestone. Issues are logged, photographed, and closed before the next trade starts.

  • Coordinated project management

    Programme, procurement, cost, and site activity are tracked together — so trade interfaces and long-lead items do not become surprises.

Our process

A clear path, from first
conversation to handover.

Every project is different, but a predictable process makes the work easier to follow. Here is the shape of a typical engagement.

  • Consultation

    A site walk, a conversation about priorities, and an honest view of budget and feasibility.

  • Planning and Design

    Drawings, specifications, and permit documentation coordinated with your designer and consultants.

  • Budgeting and Quotation

    A transparent budget with allowances, exclusions, and a schedule set out in writing.

  • Construction and Supervision

    A supervised site, weekly reporting, and controlled handling of changes and RFIs.

  • Quality Inspection

    Milestone checks against approved drawings, with issues logged and closed.

  • Project Handover

    Manuals, warranties, as-builts, and a final walkthrough before keys change hands.
