Our process

A clear path, from first conversation to handover.

Every project is different, but the sequence does not have to be a mystery. Here is what happens, who is responsible, and what you receive at each stage.

Six stages

The sequence,
in plain language.

Durations vary with scope, permitting, and material lead times. We confirm a project-specific programme in writing before construction begins.

  • Consultation

    Site walk, brief, constraints, and an honest budget conversation.

  • Planning and Design

    Drawings, engineering, specifications, and permit documentation.

  • Budgeting and Quotation

    Scope priced line by line, with allowances, exclusions, and schedule.

  • Construction and Supervision

    A supervised site, weekly reporting, and controlled change management.

  • Quality Inspection

    Checks at each milestone, with a photographed issue log.

  • Project Handover

    Manuals, warranties, as-builts, and a final room-by-room walkthrough.

What to expect

Each stage,
explained.

Construction has a rhythm. Knowing who decides what, and when, is the difference between a calm project and a reactive one.

Consultation

Week 0–1. A site walk and a candid conversation about scope, budget, and timing.

We visit the site, review any existing drawings or reports, and ask what success looks like for you. If the budget and the brief do not meet, we say so early, while changing course is still inexpensive. If the project is a fit, we outline the route to a proposal.

  • Site walk and existing-conditions review
  • Budget range and feasibility note
  • Permit and approval overview
  • Proposed delivery route
  • You provide: address, property info, target budget, priorities
  • We provide: written next-step plan and fee proposal

Planning and Design

Weeks 2–8, depending on scope. Drawings and documentation become buildable.

We coordinate your architect, structural engineer, and other consultants, and we review drawings for constructability before they go to permit. Decisions about layout, materials, and services are recorded in a decision log so nothing is agreed twice.

  • Coordinated permit drawing set
  • Structural and civil coordination
  • Finish and fixture schedule
  • Decision log with dates and owners
  • You provide: design direction, approvals, selections
  • We provide: permit-ready documentation and review comments

Budgeting and Quotation

Weeks 6–10. The scope is priced line by line, not as a single lump sum.

Our estimate separates labour, materials, subcontracts, permits, contingency, and general conditions. Allowances are stated where selections are still open, and exclusions are written down. You see the schedule and the cash-flow forecast before signing.

  • Line-item construction estimate
  • Allowances and exclusions schedule
  • Construction programme
  • Draft contract and payment schedule
  • You provide: final scope confirmation
  • We provide: quotation valid for 30 days

Construction and Supervision

Duration set by scope. A named superintendent runs the site every day.

Mobilization begins with safety orientation, utility locates, and protection of existing surfaces. You receive a weekly report with progress photos, completed work, upcoming activities, open decisions, and any cost or schedule impact. Changes require written approval before they proceed.

  • Daily site supervision
  • Weekly progress report and photos
  • RFI and submittal tracking
  • Change orders with cost and time impact
  • You provide: access, decisions, timely selections
  • We provide: a safe, clean, coordinated site

Quality Inspection

At each milestone and again at closeout. Work is checked, not assumed.

Framing, weatherproofing, mechanical, electrical, and finish stages are inspected against approved drawings. Third-party inspectors are scheduled where the jurisdiction requires them. Any defect is photographed, assigned, and closed before the next trade covers it up.

  • Milestone inspection checklists
  • Photographed issue log
  • Third-party inspection coordination
  • Punch list with closeout evidence
  • You provide: access for inspections and walkthroughs
  • We provide: signed inspection records

Project Handover

One to two weeks. Documentation, training, and a final walkthrough.

Before final payment, we walk every room with you, demonstrate equipment, and hand over manuals, warranties, and as-built drawings. A 12-month workmanship warranty begins at substantial completion, with a single point of contact for any issue.

  • Room-by-room completion walkthrough
  • Operations and maintenance manuals
  • Warranty register and as-builts
  • 12-month workmanship warranty
  • You provide: final walkthrough and sign-off
  • We provide: complete closeout package

Durations and approval requirements vary by project and jurisdiction. Nothing on this page constitutes a fixed programme or a guarantee of a particular outcome.
