Residential Construction

Custom homes, additions, ADUs, and multi-unit housing built with the same supervision standard we apply to commercial work.

We build for owners who want to understand the process, not just the finished room. That starts with a realistic preconstruction budget, a buildable drawing set, and a schedule that reflects permit timelines in Portland and the surrounding jurisdictions.

Our residential team handles everything from foundation and framing through envelope, mechanical coordination, finishes, and the final warranty walkthrough. For occupied homes, we phase work, protect the living space, and agree daily clean-up standards before demolition begins.

  • Custom single-family homes
  • Additions and second-storey builds
  • ADUs and garages
  • Multi-unit and townhome projects
  • Foundation and structural repairs
  • Decks, porches, and exterior envelopes

Commercial Construction

Offices, retail, hospitality, and light industrial work delivered around leases, operating hours, and building rules.

Commercial projects succeed or fail on coordination. We plan around landlord approvals, ADA requirements, fire-life-safety reviews, and the reality that your business may need to keep trading through the build.

Our team produces a phased schedule with after-hours and weekend work priced up front, so there are no surprises when a noisy task has to move. We coordinate directly with property managers, building engineers, and your IT and security vendors.

  • Tenant improvements and fit-outs
  • Office and workplace renovations
  • Retail and hospitality build-outs
  • Light industrial and warehouse works
  • ADA and life-safety upgrades
  • Building envelope and common-area work

Renovation and Remodeling

Kitchens, basements, structural openings, and whole-building renewals, with careful work around existing conditions.

Renovation is where planning pays for itself. We survey existing conditions before pricing, open exploratory areas where drawings are uncertain, and carry a defined contingency rather than pretending old buildings are predictable.

For older Portland homes, we coordinate structural engineering, lead and asbestos testing, and historic review where required. For occupied houses, we agree dust control, protective measures, and a room-by-room sequence before work starts.

  • Kitchen and bathroom remodels
  • Basement and attic conversions
  • Structural openings and beam replacements
  • Whole-home renovations
  • Adaptive reuse and seismic upgrades
  • Exterior siding, windows, and roofing

Civil Engineering and Infrastructure

Site grading, drainage, utilities, retaining structures, and paving — the work that lets everything above it perform.

We take on civil scopes as part of a larger build or as standalone site packages. Our focus is sequencing: getting erosion control and temporary access right first, then underground utilities, then structural work, then final grades and paving.

Projects are coordinated with civil engineers, geotechnical consultants, and local jurisdiction inspectors. Stormwater design, utility locate, and winter construction planning are addressed before mobilization rather than after a failed inspection.

  • Site clearing, grading, and excavation
  • Stormwater, sewer, and water utilities
  • Retaining walls and shoring
  • Erosion and sediment control
  • Driveways, parking, and paving
  • Sidewalks, curbs, and ramps
Request a civil quote See infrastructure work

Architectural Planning and Design

Feasibility, concept design, permit drawings, and interior documentation developed with buildability in mind.

Our design work is led by Priya Raman and coordinated with licensed architects and engineers where a project requires them. We are candid about what a site can support, what a budget will buy, and which approvals will shape the programme.

Early packages include a zoning and site review, an options study, and an order-of-magnitude budget. From there we develop permit drawings, finish schedules, and construction documentation that our own site team can price and build.

  • Feasibility and zoning review
  • Concept and schematic design
  • Permit drawing coordination
  • Interior layouts and finish schedules
  • Consultant and engineer coordination
  • Preconstruction cost studies

Construction Project Management

Owner-side management for clients who need scheduling, procurement, cost control, and site coordination without a general contract.

Some owners already have a builder but need an independent team to keep the programme honest. Others are managing multiple trades directly. We provide a named project manager, a live budget and schedule, and weekly reporting against both.

Our management service covers procurement support, bid leveling, contract administration, RFI and submittal tracking, change control, and closeout documentation. We can also supervise an existing site team on a monthly retainer.

  • Budget development and cost control
  • Procurement and bid leveling
  • Master scheduling and updates
  • RFI, submittal, and change tracking
  • Weekly progress reporting
  • Closeout and warranty administration
