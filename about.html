Our story

Built on the quiet
fundamentals.

Northform started in 2009 with two builders, a pickup truck, and a belief that most construction problems are coordination problems in disguise.

Our first projects were small renovations in inner Southeast Portland. Clients kept asking the same thing: can you help us understand what is actually going to happen? That question shaped the company.

Today we deliver new homes, multi-unit housing, commercial fit-outs, adaptive reuse, and civil site work. We still work the same way: understand the constraints early, document what has been agreed, and keep the site team connected to the design team.

Our project managers, estimators, and superintendents are full-time staff, not brokers. The person who prices your project is in the room when it is handed over.

Mission statement

To deliver considered construction through disciplined coordination, honest communication, and respect for the people who build and use it.

Leadership

The people
accountable for the work.

Safety & quality

Safe sites.
Checked work.

Safety and quality are managed on paper and verified on site. Both are led by Daniel Cho and reported to every client each week.

OSHA 30 certified supervision · documented orientations · weekly site audits

  • Site-specific safety plans

    Every project gets a written plan covering access, fall protection, utilities, public protection, and emergency procedures before crews mobilize.

  • Documented orientations

    All workers and visitors complete a site orientation. Daily pre-task checks and weekly toolbox talks are logged and available to clients.

  • Stop-work authority

    Anyone on a Northform site can stop work over an unsafe condition, without approval and without penalty.

  • Milestone quality checks

    Framing, waterproofing, mechanical, and finish stages are inspected against approved drawings before the next trade proceeds.

  • A real punch list

    Closeout includes a room-by-room walkthrough, a photographed issue log, and a signed completion record before final payment.
