How hiring works

A short process,
clearly explained.

Every applicant hears back, including the ones we cannot take forward. Here is the sequence, who you meet, and roughly how long each stage takes.

Stage one

Application review

A person reads your CV and note against the role requirements. You get a confirmation when it arrives, and a decision either way.

Within 5 business days

Stage two

Introductory call

A 30-minute conversation with the hiring manager covering the scope of the role, the pay range, the schedule, and your questions.

30 minutes, by phone

Stage three

Team interview

Meet the people you would work with daily. Depending on the role, this includes walking a project, a drawing review, or a short estimating exercise.

60–90 minutes, in person

Stage four

Offer and onboarding

A written offer stating pay, benefits, and start date, followed by a first-week plan, safety orientation, and the tools and PPE you need.

Usually 1–2 weeks

Applicant questions

Before you
apply.

Practical answers about applications, working arrangements, and what happens to your CV.

What should I include in my application?

A CV in PDF, DOC, or DOCX format up to 5 MB, the role you are applying for, and a short cover note. A portfolio or LinkedIn link is optional and only expected where the role lists it. There is no separate written test at application stage.

How long does the process take?

Most hires move from application to offer in two to four weeks. You will receive an acknowledgement within five business days, and a decision either way once the shortlist is confirmed.

Is the work on site, in the office, or hybrid?

Field roles are site-based in the Portland metro and begin early. Estimating and preconstruction roles work mainly from the office at 1420 NW 14th Avenue, with site visits as projects require. We do not currently offer fully remote positions, because the work depends on being close to drawings and to site.

Do you sponsor work visas?

Not at present. Applicants must be authorised to work in the United States. If your situation changes, a general application is still welcome.

Can I apply without a construction degree?

Yes. Each listing states the experience and qualifications it actually needs. Site supervision and estimating both have routes in through trade experience, and we consider equivalent practical experience alongside formal qualifications.

Do you accept applications from recruiters?

We work directly with applicants. Unsolicited CVs from agencies are not accepted and no fee is owed on them, so please apply yourself or email the careers address.

What happens to my CV after I apply?

Your application is used only for recruitment and is kept for 12 months, then deleted. You can ask us to remove it sooner at any time by emailing careers@northformbuild.com.

I need an adjustment to the interview process. Who do I ask?

Tell us when you apply or at any point after. We can send questions in advance, allow extra time, change the format, or meet somewhere step-free. Asking does not affect how your application is assessed.

Equal opportunity

Hiring on the work,
not the packaging.

Northform Construction assesses applicants on relevant experience, skills, and the requirements of the role. We do not discriminate on the basis of race, colour, religion, sex, sexual orientation, gender identity, national origin, age, disability, or veteran status.

Decisions are made by the people you would work with, not by an automated screening tool. If something in this process is a barrier for you, tell us and we will change it.
